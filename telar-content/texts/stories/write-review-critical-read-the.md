## How You as a ULA Can Support the student

Frame this practices as a strategic advantage rather than a chore by using these talking points:

-   The Lead-In: Try saying, "Reading the whole prompt is like looking at the final picture on a puzzle box; it helps you see where each of your great ideas fits best before you start building."
    
-   The Value Add: Emphasize that "Your time is your most valuable asset, and five minutes of reading now ensures that every word you write later actually counts toward your final grade."
    
-   The Strategy: Suggest that "Highlighting the action verbs in the prompt helps you visualize exactly how to demonstrate your mastery of the subject matter."
